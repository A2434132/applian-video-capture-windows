## 🛠️ Troubleshooting Common Issues

### No Audio in Recordings
Make sure "System Audio" is selected in the settings before recording. Also check that your computer's volume isn't muted and that you haven't accidentally turned down the application's volume in the Windows volume mixer.

### Recording Looks Blurry
Increase the resolution settings in the recording options before you start. Also ensure you're not recording a screen that's zoomed out too far — it's better to record a smaller area in higher quality.

### Program Won't Open
Right-click the program icon and select "Run as administrator." If it still won't open, try restarting your computer and running the installer again.

### The "Record" Button is Grayed Out
This usually means you haven't selected a recording mode. Click on "Screen," "Webcam," or "Stream" at the top first, and the button should become active.
